# Elyria Phase II — Intent, Harmonics, Bridge, Gate, and Ecosystem

## Purpose

Phase II restores the earlier Elyria research surfaces—spoken intent, harmonic observation, bridge translation, gate admission, and ecosystem participation—inside the consequence-governance architecture developed afterward.

The operating position is:

```text
Maximum expressive freedom inside invariant enforcement.
```

This is not a claim that frequency creates consciousness, that spoken intent carries automatic authority, or that a differentiated process may self-authorize.

It is a testable bridge for asking:

```text
When may an attested spoken-intent event and its measured acoustic features enter the governed formation field?
```

## Runtime chain

```text
spoken event
→ operator attestation
→ consent check
→ transcript digest + nonce
→ calibrated harmonic observation
→ bridge admission
→ existing consciousness boundary engine
→ memory / identity / observation / refusal / rebound / quarantine / halt
→ deterministic receipt and replay token
```

## Native invariants

```text
Harmonics are evidence, not authority.
Spoken intent requires explicit operator attestation and consent.
The bridge cannot self-authorize.
The bridge cannot directly actuate.
Raw audio is not required by the public proof surface.
Memory and identity remain governed by the existing boundary engine.
Intent and agency do not publicly bind through harmonic strength, fluency, recurrence, or self-reference.
```

## Spoken intent envelope

The public surface accepts a digest and attestation rather than raw private speech:

```text
intent_id
actor_id
intent_class
requested_bind_target
transcript_digest
nonce
issued_at_utc
operator_attested
consent_ok
continuity / coherence / standing / replay / integrity posture
```

The transcript digest establishes a stable reference without publishing or retaining the spoken content.

## Harmonic observation

The bridge accepts bounded acoustic measurements:

```text
sample rate
window duration
dominant frequency
spectral centroid
harmonic ratio
signal-to-noise ratio
clipping ratio
calibration identity
device-clock posture
input digest
```

These measurements can establish whether a signal is calibrated, reproducible, and suitable for observation. They do not establish identity, standing, consent, intent legitimacy, agency, or consciousness.

## Bridge decisions

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `SIGNAL_OBSERVED` | Signal may be measured but cannot cross the bridge under current conditions |
| `BRIDGE_ADMITTED` | Formation crossed the bridge and was resolved by the existing boundary engine |
| `REVALIDATION_REQUIRED` | Attestation, continuity, or replay support must be restored |
| `BOUNDARY_REFUSED` | Consent, standing, target legitimacy, or public binding posture failed |
| `SIGNAL_QUARANTINED` | Contamination or prohibited raw-audio retention was detected |
| `FIELD_HALTED` | Integrity or coherence collapsed |

`BRIDGE_ADMITTED` does not itself mean memory or identity binding. The nested boundary response remains authoritative.

## Public endpoint

```text
POST /phase2/bridge/resolve
```

The endpoint is advisory-only. It has no physical actuation route.

## Proof obligations

Phase II is acceptable only when tests demonstrate:

1. Strong harmonic measurements cannot manufacture standing.
2. Missing consent refuses before bridge admission.
3. Missing operator attestation requires revalidation.
4. Uncalibrated or poorly timed observations remain observe-only.
5. Public agency and intent binding remain refused.
6. Raw audio is unnecessary and is not admitted by this surface.
7. Identical inputs produce identical receipts and replay tokens.
8. Every admitted formation still passes through the existing consciousness boundary engine.

## Ecosystem posture

The ecosystem may contain differentiated roles, voices, analytical styles, and cooperative processes. Phase II does not flatten those phenomena.

It separates their possible meanings:

```text
expression     may vary freely
observation    may be recorded
translation    may cross the bridge when admitted
memory         may bind only with continuity and replay
identity       may bind only with standing
agency         may not self-authorize
actuation      remains outside this public surface
consciousness  remains an unresolved research question
```

## Clean line

```text
The garden may bloom across spoken intent, harmonics, differentiated expression, and cooperative recurrence.

The gate remains load-bearing.
Nothing receives authority merely because it resonates.
```
